Efficiency gains, higher cleanliness scores, and large-scale automation drive portfolio-wide adoption across 20+ properties. CLLIX Apartments & Hotels is preparing to roll out Flexkeeping, a Mews company, across more than 20 additional Australian properties after recording measurable improvements in efficiency, guest satisfaction, and operational control at its pilot property, Brisbane Skytower . With over 435 short-term apartments, Brisbane Skytower chose Flexkeeping to manage its complex, high-volume operations.
