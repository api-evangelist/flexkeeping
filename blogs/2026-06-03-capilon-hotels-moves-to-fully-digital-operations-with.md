The London hotel group reports a 343% ROI following the rollout across all four properties. Capilon Hotels has completed the rollout of Flexkeeping, a Mews company , across its four central London properties, consolidating housekeeping, maintenance and front office operations onto a single platform after years of running on largely manual, paper-based processes. The group, which manages 199 rooms across properties in central London, went live earlier this year.
