New integration automatically converts online reviews into actionable housekeeping and maintenance tasks. Flexkeeping, a Mews company, has launched an integration with MARA Solutions, enabling hotels to turn guest feedback into immediate operational action. The integration connects MARA’s reputation management platform with Flexkeeping’s operation system.
